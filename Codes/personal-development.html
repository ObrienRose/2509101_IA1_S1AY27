Personal Development

My Growth and Reflection

The Richest Man in Babylon teaches that financial success can grow from good habits and wise decisions. The chapters “Seven Cures for a Lean Purse” and “The Five Laws of Gold” discuss saving, controlling spending, investing carefully, and improving one’s ability to earn. As a Computer Science student, I found these lessons useful because they can help me manage money while preparing for my future career.

Jump to: Seven Cures for a Lean Purse | Five Laws of Gold | My Biggest Takeaway

Seven Cures for a Lean Purse

This chapter presents seven habits for improving a person’s financial situation. It explains the importance of saving, controlling spending, making savings grow, protecting money, planning for future income, and improving one’s ability to earn.

Key Points

  • Save at least one-tenth of what you earn.
  • Control your spending and avoid unnecessary expenses.
  • Put your savings to work so they can grow.
  • Protect your money from risky investments.
  • Consider making your home a useful investment.
  • Plan for future income.
  • Keep developing your ability to earn.

My Reflection

I learned that saving should become a habit, even when I do not earn much. As a university student, I can start by saving part of any money I receive from part-time work or family. I also believe that learning programming languages and building projects can improve my future career opportunities and earning potential.

Five Laws of Gold

This chapter shows that building wealth takes knowledge and careful decisions. It explains with Arkad's son that people should save regularly, use their money wisely, seek advice from people with experience, and avoid investments they do not understand.

Key Points

  • Save a portion of your income.
  • Find sensible ways to help your savings grow.
  • Ask experienced people for advice about handling money.
  • Be cautious about investing in things you do not understand.
  • Avoid offers that promise quick or unrealistic profits.

My Reflection

This chapter reminded me that knowledge is just as important as money. There are many online investments that promise fast profits, so I should research carefully and seek reliable advice before investing. In my future career as a software developer, I want to make thoughtful financial decisions and avoid rushing into choices I do not understand.

My Biggest Takeaway

My biggest takeaway is that good financial habits can begin before a person earns a large salary. Saving, budgeting, learning, and making careful decisions are habits I can start building now. I hope they will help me work toward financial stability and success in my future career.

Reference

Clason, G. S. (1988). The richest man in Babylon. Signet. (Original work published 1926). View the book
