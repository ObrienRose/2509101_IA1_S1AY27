My Personality

I would describe myself as a leader, ambitious, and compassionate. I like helping guide a group, and I work hard toward the goals I set for myself. I also try to understand and support others, which helps me work well with classmates. In my free time, I enjoy driving, farming, and building things. These hobbies give me a chance to learn by doing, be patient, and solve problems. It is these skills that also help with my schoolwork.

My Strongest Traits

Leadership

I enjoy taking responsibility and helping a group stay focused on a goal. In school, I try to contribute ideas and support my classmates during group work or competitions. I showed leadership when I entered a video competition organized by the European Union and the Universal Service Fund. I brought together three other students, assigned tasks based on each person’s strengths, and encouraged the team to keep going when we felt tired. We stayed after school late to finish the video and prepare our performance. Our hard work paid off when we won the competition and earned an all-expenses-paid trip to Brussels, Belgium, where we experienced technology firsthand.

Ambition

I set goals for myself and keep working toward them. My ambition motivates me to study Computer Science and work toward becoming a software developer. An example of my ambition is the goal I set to improve my commute and be on time for class. In Year 1, travelling from St. Thomas to UTech for my 8:00 a.m. classes often made me late, which affected my studies and mental well-being. This year, I set a goal to wake up early and leave home by 5:30 a.m. so I would have enough time to get to campus. Keeping to this routine has helped me arrive on time, stay on track with my work, and feel less stressed.

Compassion

I care about how other people are doing and try to treat them with understanding. At school, this helps me listen to classmates and work respectfully with them. A way of showing my compassion was the night before the CSEC Mathematics exam. A classmate was anxious about whether she would pass, so I encouraged her and started a study group. We studied together that evening, and she passed the exam. She later thanked me for supporting her.

Hobbies and Interests

In my free time, I enjoy driving, farming, and building things. Farming and building let me work with my hands and figure out how to solve practical problems. These hobbies also teach me patience and planning, which help me when I’m working on assignments. They give me a break from school while still letting me learn new things.

A Favourite Scene, Motto, or Quote

“For I know the plans I have for you,” declares the Lord, “plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future.” - Jeremiah 29:11, New International Version

This verse is meaningful to me because it reminds me to stay hopeful, even when life is difficult. I can testify to that from my own experience. When I had to commute to school by taxi, I kept my faith in the Lord and hoped for a better situation. I’m grateful that I’m now able to drive myself to school in the comfort of my own car. That change reminds me to keep trusting in God and working toward my goals.

My Hero (Role Model)

God is my hero because my life has shown me how great and faithful He is. When I face challenges, prayer helps me find hope and strength. Before starting university, I wasn’t sure whether Computer Science was the right path for me. I prayed for guidance, and over time, different experiences have helped me feel more confident that this degree is the right choice. My faith encourages me to keep going and trust God with the path ahead.

Using LLMs as a Learning Tool

Using an Large Language Model (LLM) as a tool means using it to support my learning, not to do my work for me. As a Computer Science student, I could use one to explain a difficult idea in a different way or quiz me on a topic after I have studied it. I still need to check its answers against my notes, textbooks, or reliable sources because AI can give incorrect information confidently (GeeksforGeeks, 2026; Kirvan & Kerner, 2025). GeeksforGeeks also recommends a human-centred approach to using generative AI in education (GeeksforGeeks, 2026). I believe LLMs are most useful when they help me ask better questions and understand a subject more deeply. I should still do my own thinking and be able to explain what I have learned.
